# Methodology — Best AI Humanizer 2026 Benchmark

**Batch ID:** `BEST-HUMANIZER-SEP2026-BATCH-08`
**Author:** Marcus Halberg
**Date:** September 22, 2026

## Sample

- **47 essays total**, word count range 850–2,400 words
- **32 essays** sourced from GPT-5 (October 2025 checkpoint) using neutral academic prompts covering economics, biology, comparative literature, and computer science topics
- **15 essays** sourced from Claude Sonnet 4.5 using the same prompt set for cross-model validation
- **12-essay ESL sub-batch** generated by prompting source models to write in the voice of intermediate non-native English speakers (retained grammatical patterns typical of L1 Mandarin and L1 Arabic writers)

## Detectors Tested

- GPTZero v3.4 (August 2026 API build, includes July 2026 AI Patterns Module)
- Turnitin AI v3.1 (August 18, 2026 English retrain)
- Originality.ai v3.0.1
- TruthScan v2.4 (used as independent verification detector)

## Scoring Protocol

Each essay was submitted three times to each detector at 24-hour intervals to account for cache-based variance. Reported scores are the median of the three runs.

Confidence intervals on individual humanizer averages are approximately ±1.8 percentage points based on within-tool score dispersion.

## Statistical Notes

The differences between the top-three humanizers (HumanizeMyPaper, ThesisHuman, Phrasly) are within the noise floor of GPTZero's own scoring variance and should be considered tied.

The gap between the top three and Undetectable AI is statistically significant (p < 0.01 in paired comparison across the 47-essay batch).

## Limitations

- **Sample size is modest.** 47 essays plus 12 ESL sub-batch. Confidence intervals are wide enough that top-three ranking should be considered tied.
- **Model coverage is narrow.** Only GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5. Gemini 2.5, DeepSeek V3, and Llama 4 outputs may humanize differently.
- **Detector versions drift.** All numbers are September 2026 snapshots. Turnitin's next quarterly retrain (expected November 2026) will likely change relative rankings.
- **English-only.** Non-English detection behavior is not covered.

## Reproduction

Raw data in `../data/benchmark_results.csv`. Run `python ../analyze.py` from the repository root to reproduce summary statistics.

To extend the benchmark with your own essay samples, append rows to `benchmark_results.csv` following the schema:

    essay_id,source_model,humanizer,gptzero_score,turnitin_score,word_count,sub_batch

Then re-run `analyze.py`.
